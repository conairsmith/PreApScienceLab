📖 Part 1 Concept Guide — Food Webs & Trophic Cascades

Use these cards to check your Part 1 guided notes on Day 1!

1. Producer (Autotroph) 🌿

An organism that makes its own food using sunlight through photosynthesis. Examples: Giant Kelp, willow trees, grass, phytoplankton.

2. Consumer (Heterotroph) 🦦🐟

Gets energy by eating other organisms:
• Primary Consumer (Herbivore): Eats plants / producers.
• Secondary Consumer: Eats primary consumers.
• Apex Predator: Top predator with no natural enemies.

3. The 10% Energy Rule ⚡🔥

Only 10% of energy moves up from one trophic level to the next! The other 90% is used for life processes (moving, growing) and lost into the environment as heat.

4. Keystone Species 🏛️🦦

Like the center stone at the top of a stone arch, a keystone species holds an entire ecosystem together. Removing it causes a massive collapse—even if there aren't very many of them!

5. Trophic Cascade 🁢🌊

A chain-reaction domino effect where adding or removing a predator at the top of a food web causes dramatic changes all the way down to the plants at the bottom.

6. Carrying Capacity 📈🦌

The maximum population size an ecosystem can safely support over time without running out of food, water, or shelter.
